01 Who We Are & What This Policy Covers

This Privacy Policy explains what limited information T1or Studios collects, uses, displays, and deletes when you interact with:

  • Skyfortress Verification — our Roblox OAuth 2.0 identity verification flow used to confirm ownership of a Roblox account.
  • Skyfortress Discord Bot — the official bot operating inside the Skyfortress Military Facility Discord server.
  • Skyfortress Archive & Playtester Application Tracker — our static website at https://t1orstudios.github.io/ and public recruitment status ledger at /tracker/.

We follow strict data minimization: we collect only the minimum public account identifiers needed to verify your Roblox account in Discord and display public playtester application status.

02 Roblox OAuth 2.0 Scopes & Token Handling

When you verify your Roblox account through Skyfortress Verification, you sign in directly on Roblox's official OAuth 2.0 authorization page. We request only the following two read-only scopes:

OAuth Scope Access Data Returned by Roblox How It Is Used
openid Read-Only Roblox User ID (sub) Uniquely links your verified Roblox account to your Discord account and prevents duplicate linking.
profile Read-Only Public Roblox Username, Display Name, and public Avatar URL Confirms your public identity for the Verified role, optional Discord nickname sync, and /whois lookup.
OAuth Access & Refresh Tokens Discarded Short-lived OAuth bearer/refresh credentials returned during callback Used once in memory to call Roblox's userinfo endpoint, then immediately discarded. Never written to disk, logged, or stored.
Passwords, Write Access, Robux, Inventory, Friends, Email Never Requested None We never see your Roblox password and never request write permissions, Robux/billing data, inventory, friends list, group write access, or email via Roblox OAuth 2.0.

Zero Persistent Token Storage

Immediately after Skyfortress Verification exchanges your one-time OAuth code and reads your public identity from Roblox's userinfo endpoint, the OAuth access token and any refresh token are discarded from memory. No OAuth tokens are ever stored on disk or in any database.

03 Data Stored by the Skyfortress Discord Bot

After successful Roblox OAuth 2.0 verification, the Skyfortress Discord Bot records your verification link in its verification store (verified_users.json). That record contains only the following five fields:

  • Discord User ID — your numeric Discord snowflake identifier.
  • Roblox User ID — the numeric Roblox sub returned by Roblox OAuth.
  • Roblox Username — your public Roblox username at the time of verification.
  • Roblox Display Name — your public Roblox display name at the time of verification.
  • Verification UTC Timestamp — the date and time (UTC) when verification completed.

Sole Purpose of Verification Data

This record is used exclusively to operate server verification features:

  • Assigning the Verified role in the Skyfortress Military Facility Discord server.
  • Setting an optional verified server nickname (where enabled and permitted by Discord role hierarchy).
  • Enforcing the account takeover lock so another Discord user cannot hijack or re-link a Roblox account already verified to you.
  • Answering the /whois slash command inside the server to display which public Roblox profile is linked to a Discord member.

We do not log private Discord direct messages, read unrelated server message history, or store payment information, physical addresses, or passwords.

04 Public Playtester Application Tracker & Archive

Skyfortress Archive (https://t1orstudios.github.io/) is a static website that can be browsed without creating an account or signing in.

When you submit a Playtester Application for Skyfortress Military Facility, a sanitized public status entry is published on the Playtester Application Tracker (/tracker/) so applicants can check review progress. The public tracker displays only:

  • Application ID (for example, SF-004)
  • Roblox Username
  • Submission Date (calendar date only)
  • Review Status (such as Pending Review, Accepted, Waitlisted, or Rejected)
  • Official Roblox Avatar Headshot loaded directly from Roblox's public CDN (tr.rbxcdn.com)

Never Published: Applicant email addresses, private application form responses and essays, internal staff notes, and review scores are strictly excluded from /tracker/ and are never published on Skyfortress Archive.

05 Data Sharing, Cookies & Third-Party Services

  • Zero Sale of Data: T1or Studios never sells, rents, licenses, or trades user data to any third party for any reason.
  • Zero Ad Trackers or Analytics Cookies: Skyfortress Archive, /tracker/, and Skyfortress Verification do not use advertising trackers, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics cookies.
  • Essential Platform Connections Only: Verification interacts only with Roblox (OAuth 2.0 identity handshake and public avatar headshots from tr.rbxcdn.com) and Discord (bot commands, Verified role, and nickname updates). Static site pages are hosted on GitHub Pages and load fonts from Google Fonts, which receive standard web request metadata (such as IP address and browser user-agent) under their own privacy policies.

06 Data Retention

Data Category Storage Location Retention Period
Roblox OAuth Access / Refresh Tokens Memory only (never persisted) 0 seconds after lookup — discarded immediately after fetching userinfo.
Discord–Roblox Verification Link verified_users.json Kept only while linked; deleted immediately when you run /unverify or Staff runs /force-unverify.
Public Playtester Tracker Entry Sanitized public registry feed (/tracker/) Retained while recruitment records are active, or removed promptly upon request via the Discord support desk.

07 Your Rights & Instant Data Deletion

You stay in full control of your verification link and may remove it at any time with zero waiting period:

  • Self-Service Instant Unlink (/unverify): Run /unverify in the Skyfortress Military Facility Discord server at any time. The bot immediately deletes your entry from verified_users.json, removes the Verified role, and releases the takeover lock.
  • Staff-Assisted Removal (/force-unverify): If you have left the server, lost access to your Discord account, or want a Playtester Application Tracker entry removed, open a support ticket in the Skyfortress Military Facility Discord server and request Staff to run /force-unverify or purge your tracker entry.
  • Roblox Connected Apps: You may also revoke Skyfortress Verification at any time from your Roblox Account Settings (Security → Connected Apps), though we do not retain any OAuth tokens to begin with.

08 Minimum Age Requirement (13+)

Skyfortress Verification, the Skyfortress Discord Bot, and playtester applications are intended strictly for users aged 13 years or older (or the higher minimum digital consent age required in your country). You must meet both Discord's and Roblox's minimum age and eligibility requirements to use our verification services.

We do not knowingly collect or store information from children under 13. If you believe an underage account has been verified or listed on the tracker, please notify Staff via a support ticket so we can delete the record promptly.

09 Security, Policy Updates & Contact

Security: All web and OAuth traffic uses HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit, verification uses read-only scopes with immediate token disposal, and access to verified_users.json is restricted to the bot runtime and authorized T1or Studios administrators.

Changes to This Policy: If we update how Skyfortress Verification, the Discord Bot, or Skyfortress Archive handles data, we will publish the revised policy at https://t1orstudios.github.io/privacy/ and update the Effective Date at the top of this page.

Contact Us: For privacy inquiries, data access, or deletion requests (including Staff /force-unverify), contact T1or Studios by opening a support ticket at the Skyfortress Military Facility Discord server support desk.
